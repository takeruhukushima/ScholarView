ScholarView provides a desktop-class workspace designed so researchers can write, publish, and engage in dialogue seamlessly.

## 1. Three-column workspace

A functional layout that supports an efficient research workflow.

- **Sidebar (FileTree)**: Manage paper sections (Markdown/TeX), images, and BibTeX files. Supports drag-and-drop import and reorganization.
- **Editor Panel (Workspace Editor)**: The main area for writing and managing your paper.
- **Right Panel (Discussion & Preview)**: Check the final rendered output and follow real-time deliberation threads on Bluesky.

## 2. Hybrid block editor

ScholarView's editor uses a custom block-based design that combines the expressiveness of Markdown and LaTeX.

- **TeX and Markdown support**: Real-time math rendering with KaTeX.
- **Automatic citations**: Type `\cite{}` or `[@citekey]` while writing, and candidates are suggested from the BibTeX files in your workspace, with citation numbers assigned automatically.
- **Drag-and-drop asset management**: Drop an image file onto the editor and it is automatically uploaded to your PDS and embedded in the paper by CID.

## 3. Bluesky sync (Broadcast & Sync)

Share your paper with the academic community in one click.

- **Broadcast**: When you save a paper to your PDS, a post announcing its publication is automatically posted to Bluesky.
- **Synchronize**: Reactions on Bluesky (likes, reposts) are reflected in your workspace in real time.
- **Record management**: Update or delete published papers — including deleting the corresponding Bluesky post — directly from the editor.

## 4. Inline deliberation (Contextual Annotations)

Enables in-depth discussion focused on a specific passage of a paper.

- **Highlight & comment**: Select text in the paper preview to start a Bluesky reply about that passage.
- **Quote context preserved**: To make clear which text a comment refers to, the quoted context is automatically embedded in the Bluesky external link card.
- **How it looks on Bluesky**: In the regular Bluesky app it appears as a post with a link quoting the paper; in ScholarView it expands into an inline comment with the relevant passage highlighted.

## 5. Onboarding guide

To help you get started with a complex decentralized system without getting lost, a **Tutorial Tour** walks you through the main features step by step on your first visit.
